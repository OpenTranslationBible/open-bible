# Sabuurradii 53

### Agaasimaha muusikada; sida Mahalath. Maskil oo ah kii Daa'uud.

## Sabuurradii 53:1

> Nacasku wuxuu qalbigiisa ku yidhi, “Ilaah ma jiro.”  
> Way fasaadiyeen, oo waxay sameeyeen dulmi karaahiyo ah;  
> Mid wanaag sameeyaa ma jiro.

---

## Sabuurradii 53:2

> Eebbe wuxuu ka fiiriyey samada korkooda bini-aadmiga  
> Si uu u arko haddii ay jiraan kuwo wax garanaya,  
> Kuwa Eebbe doondoonaya.

## Sabuurradii 53:3

> Dhammaantood way leexdeen; kulligood way wada fasahaadeen;  
> Mid wanaag sameeyaa ma jiro, xataa hal midna.

---

## Sabuurradii 53:4

> Ma garanayaan kuwa dulmiga shaqeeyaa,

> Kuwa dadkayga sida kibis u cuna,  
> Oo aan Eebbe ku baryin?

## Sabuurradii 53:5

> Way cabsadeen iyagoo aan sabab loo cabsan jirin;  
> Waayo, Eebbe wuxuu kala firdhiyey lafihii kuwa kugu soo duulay;  
> Waxaad ku ceebaysay, maxaa yeelay, Eebbe waa diiday iyaga.

---

## Sabuurradii 53:6

> Bal inay badbaadinta Israa'iil Siyoon ka timaado!  
> Markii Eebbe soo celiyo dadkaagiisa nasiibkooda,  
> Yacquub wuu farxi doonaa, Israa'iilna wuu ku reyreyi doonaa.
