# Sabuur 83

### Hees, Sabuur Asaf.

## Sabuur 83:1

> Eebbe, ha aamusin;  
> Ha aamusin, Eebbow, ha degdegin.

## Sabuur 83:2

> Waayo, cadowgaagu qaylo ayay ku jiraan,  
> Kuwa kaa soo horjeedaana way is kiciyeen.

## Sabuur 83:3

> Waxay maleegaan dhagar ka dhan ah dadkaaga,  
> Waxayna si wadajir ah u shirqoolaan kuwa aad jeceshahay.

## Sabuur 83:4

> Waxay ku dhawaaqeen, “Kaalay, aan iyaga qaran ahaan u baabi'inno,  
> Si magaca Israa'iil loo illoobo.”

---

## Sabuur 83:5

> Waayo, waxay ku midoobeen hal ujeedo;  
> Waxay sameeyeen isbahaysi kaa soo horjeeda:

## Sabuur 83:6

> Teendhooyinka Edom iyo reer Ismaaciil,  
> Moo'aab iyo reer Hagar;

## Sabuur 83:7

> Dadka Gebal, Cammoon, iyo Caamaaleq,  
> Filistiin oo ay weheliyaan dadka Turos;

## Sabuur 83:8

> Asuuriyahuna iyagaa la midoobay;  
> Waxay taageero siiyeen farcanka Luud.

> Selah

---

## Sabuur 83:9

> La dhaqan sidaad Midyaan ula dhaqantay,  
> Sidaad Sisera iyo Jaabiin ugu dhaqantay webiga Qishoon,

## Sabuur 83:10

> Kuwaas oo dhammaadkoodii ku kulmay Een-door,  
> Iyagoo noqday qashinka dhulka.

## Sabuur 83:11

> Amiirradooda ha noqdeen sida Ooreb iyo Seeb,  
> Iyo madaxooda sida Sebax iyo Salmunna,

## Sabuur 83:12

> Kuwaas oo yidhi, “Aan hantinno  
> Beeraha Eebbe.”

---

## Sabuur 83:13

> Eebbow, ka dhig sida boodhka wareegaya,  
> Sida buunshaha dabayshu kaxayso.

## Sabuur 83:14

> Sida dabka kaynta cuna,  
> Iyo sida ololka buuraha shidaya,

## Sabuur 83:15

> Sidaas oo kale ku eryo duufaantaada,  
> Oo ku cabsi geli duufaankaaga.

## Sabuur 83:16

> Wajigooda ceeb ku dabool,  
> Si ay magacaaga, Sayidow, u doondoonaan.

---

## Sabuur 83:17

> Ha noqdeen kuwo weligood ceeboobay oo niyad jabay,  
> Oo ha noqdeen kuwo la bahdilay oo baaba'a,

## Sabuur 83:18

> Si ay u aqoonsadaan in adiga keligaa, magacaagu yahay Sayidka,  
> Ayaa ugu Sarreeya dhulka oo dhan.
