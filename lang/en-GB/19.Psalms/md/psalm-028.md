# Psalm 28

### A Psalm of David.

## Psalm 28:1

> To You, Lord, I call;  
>  my rock, do not be deaf to me,  
>  for if You are silent to me,  
>  I will become like those who go down to the pit.

## Psalm 28:2

> Listen to my pleas when I cry out for Your help,  
>  when I lift my hands toward Your sacred sanctuary.

---

## Psalm 28:3

> Do not drag me away with the wicked and with those who practice injustice,  
>  who speak peace with their neighbours,  
>  while evil is in their hearts.

## Psalm 28:4

> Repay them according to their deeds and according to the wickedness of their actions;  
>  repay them for the work of their hands;  
>  give them what they deserve.

---

## Psalm 28:5

> Because they do not consider the works of the Lord nor the deeds of His hands,  
>  He will tear them down and not build them up.

---

## Psalm 28:6

> Blessed be the Lord,  
>  for He has listened to the voice of my pleas.

## Psalm 28:7

> The Lord is my strength and my shield;  
>  my heart trusts in Him, and I am helped;  
>  therefore my heart rejoices,  
>  and with my song I shall thank Him.

## Psalm 28:8

> The Lord is the strength of His people,  
>  and He is a stronghold of salvation for His anointed.

## Psalm 28:9

> Deliver Your people and grant favour to Your heritage;  
>  guide them and support them always.
