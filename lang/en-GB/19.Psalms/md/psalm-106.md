# Psalm 106

## Psalm 106:1

> Praise the Lord!

---

>  Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good;  
>  for His mercy is everlasting.

---

## Psalm 106:2

> Who can speak of the mighty deeds of the Lord,  
>  or proclaim all His praise?

## Psalm 106:3

> Blessed are those who keep justice,  
>  who practise righteousness at all times!

---

## Psalm 106:4

> Remember me, Lord, in Your favour toward Your people;  
>  visit me with Your salvation,

## Psalm 106:5

> that I may witness the prosperity of Your chosen ones,  
>  that I may share in the joy of Your nation,  
>  that I may take pride in Your inheritance.

---

## Psalm 106:6

> We have sinned as our ancestors did,  
>  we have strayed, we have acted wickedly.

## Psalm 106:7

> Our ancestors in Egypt did not grasp Your wonders;  
>  they failed to remember Your abundant kindnesses,  
>  but rebelled by the sea, at the Red Sea.

## Psalm 106:8

> Yet He saved them for the sake of His name,  
>  to make His power known.

## Psalm 106:9

> He rebuked the Red Sea and it dried up,  
>  and He led them through the mighty waters, as through the wilderness.

## Psalm 106:10

> Thus He saved them from the hand of those who hated them,  
>  and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.

## Psalm 106:11

> The waters covered their adversaries;  
>  not one of them remained.

## Psalm 106:12

> Then they trusted His words;  
>  they sang His praise.

---

## Psalm 106:13

> They quickly forgot His works;  
>  they did not wait for His guidance,

## Psalm 106:14

> but became greedily desirous in the wilderness,  
>  and tested God in the desert.

## Psalm 106:15

> So He granted their request,  
>  but sent a wasting disease among them.

---

## Psalm 106:16

> When they envied Moses in the camp,  
>  and Aaron, the holy one of the Lord,

## Psalm 106:17

> the ground opened and took in Dathan,  
>  and enveloped the assembly of Abiram.

## Psalm 106:18

> And a fire blazed up in their company;  
>  the flame consumed the evildoers.

## Psalm 106:19

> They made a calf at Horeb,  
>  and worshipped a cast metal image.

## Psalm 106:20

> Thus they exchanged their glory for the likeness of an ox that eats grass.

## Psalm 106:21

> They forgot God their Saviour,  
>  who had accomplished great things in Egypt,

## Psalm 106:22

> miracles in the land of Ham,  
>  and awe-inspiring deeds by the Red Sea.

## Psalm 106:23

> Therefore He declared He would destroy them,  
>  had not Moses, His chosen one, stood in the breach before Him,  
>  to turn away His wrath from destroying them.

---

## Psalm 106:24

> Then they rejected the pleasant land;  
>  they did not believe His promise,

## Psalm 106:25

> but grumbled in their tents;  
>  they did not heed the voice of the Lord.

## Psalm 106:26

> Therefore He swore to them that He would let them fall in the wilderness,

## Psalm 106:27

> and that He would disperse their descendants among the nations,  
>  and scatter them in the lands.

---

## Psalm 106:28

> They also followed Baal of Peor,  
>  and ate sacrifices offered to the dead.

## Psalm 106:29

> Thus they provoked Him to anger with their deeds,  
>  and a plague broke out among them.

## Psalm 106:30

> Then Phinehas stood up and intervened,  
>  and the plague was halted.

## Psalm 106:31

> And it was credited to him as righteousness,  
>  for all generations to come.

## Psalm 106:32

> They also provoked Him to wrath at the waters of Meribah,  
>  so that it went badly for Moses because of them;

## Psalm 106:33

> because they defied His Spirit,  
>  he spoke rashly with his lips.

---

## Psalm 106:34

> They did not eliminate the peoples,  
>  as the Lord had commanded them,

## Psalm 106:35

> but they mixed with the nations and adopted their customs,

## Psalm 106:36

> and worshipped their idols,  
>  which became a trap for them.

## Psalm 106:37

> For they even sacrificed their sons and their daughters to the demons,

## Psalm 106:38

> and shed innocent blood,  
>  the blood of their sons and daughters,  
>  whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan;  
>  and the land was defiled with the blood.

## Psalm 106:39

> Thus they became unclean in their practices,  
>  and were unfaithful in their deeds.

---

## Psalm 106:40

> Therefore the anger of the Lord was kindled against His people,  
>  and He loathed His inheritance.

## Psalm 106:41

> So He delivered them into the hands of the nations,  
>  and those who hated them ruled over them.

## Psalm 106:42

> Their enemies also oppressed them,  
>  and they were subdued under their power.

## Psalm 106:43

> Many times He would rescue them;  
>  yet they were rebellious in their intentions,  
>  and they sank down into their guilt.

## Psalm 106:44

> Yet He looked upon their distress when He heard their cry;

## Psalm 106:45

> and He remembered His covenant for their sake,  
>  and relented according to the greatness of His mercy.

## Psalm 106:46

> He also made them objects of compassion in the sight of all their captors.

---

## Psalm 106:47

> Deliver us, Lord our God,  
>  and gather us from the nations,  
>  to give thanks to Your holy name and glory in Your praise.

---

## Psalm 106:48

> Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel,  
>  from everlasting to everlasting.

>  And all the people shall say, “Amen.”

>  Praise the Lord!
