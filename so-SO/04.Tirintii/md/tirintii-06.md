# Tirinta Cutubka 6

## Tirinta 6:1

Markaasaa Rabbigu mar kale la hadlay Muuse, isagoo leh,

## Tirinta 6:2

“La hadal reer binu Israa'iil oo u sheeg, ‘Marka nin ama naag ay samayso nidar gaar ah, nidarka Naasiri, si ay naftooda ugu hibeeyaan Rabbiga,

## Tirinta 6:3

waa inay ka fogaadaan khamriga iyo cabbitaanka xooggan; waa inaanay cabbin khal laga sameeyey khamri ama cabbitaan xooggan, mana cuni doonaan casiir canab ama canab cusub ama qallalan.

## Tirinta 6:4

Inta lagu jiro muddada nidarkooda, waa inaanay cunin wax ka yimaada geedka canabka, xataa iniinaha ama maqaarka.

## Tirinta 6:5

“‘Inta lagu jiro muddada nidarka Naasiri, maqaarka ma taaban doono madaxiisa. Waa inay ahaadaan quduus ilaa waqtiga ay naftooda u hibeeyaan Rabbiga la dhammeeyo; waa inay timahooda dhaadheeraan.

## Tirinta 6:6

“‘Inta lagu jiro muddada ay naftooda u hibeeyaan Rabbiga, Naasiri waa inaanu u dhowaan qof dhintay.

## Tirinta 6:7

Waa inaanay is nijaasayn aabbahood, hooyadood, walaalkood, ama walaashood marka ay dhintaan, maxaa yeelay calaamadda quduusnimadooda Eebbe ayaa madaxiisa saaran.

## Tirinta 6:8

Inta lagu jiro muddada ay naftooda u hibeeyaan, waa quduus Rabbiga.

## Tirinta 6:9

“‘Haddii qof si kedis ah ugu dhinto agtooda, iyagoo nijaasaynaya madaxa timahooda quduuska ah, waa inay xiiraan madaxa maalinta toddobaad, maalinta nadiifintooda.

## Tirinta 6:10

Maalinta siddeedaad, waa inay keensadaan laba qoolley ama laba qoolley oo yaryar wadaadka iridda teendhada kulanka.

## Tirinta 6:11

Wadaadku waa inuu mid u bixiyo sida dembi bixinta iyo mid kale sida gubashada si uu u sameeyo kafaaraggudkooda maxaa yeelay waxay dembaabeen iyagoo joogay qof dhintay agtiisa. Isla maalintaas, waa inay dib u quduusayn madaxiisa.

## Tirinta 6:12

Waa inay dib ugu hibeeyaan naftooda Rabbiga muddada buuxda ee nidarkooda oo ay keensadaan wan hal sano jir ah sida dembi bixinta. Maalmaha hore ma xisaabsan yihiin, maadaama quduusnimadoodii la nijaaseeyey.

## Tirinta 6:13

“‘Tani waa sharciga Naasiri marka muddada ay naftooda u hibeeyaan la dhammeeyo: Waa in la keeno iridda teendhada kulanka.

## Tirinta 6:14

Halkaasay ku soo bandhigi doonaan allabaryadooda Rabbiga: wan hal sano jir ah oo aan iin lahayn sida gubashada, wan hal sano jir ah oo aan iin lahayn sida dembi bixinta, iyo wan aan iin lahayn sida nabad bixinta,

## Tirinta 6:15

iyo dambiil rooti aan khamiir lahayn oo laga sameeyey bur fiican oo saliid lagu daray, iyo rooti aan khamiir lahayn oo saliid lagu faafiyey, oo ay weheliyaan allabarigooda daqiiqda iyo cabbitaanka.

## Tirinta 6:16

“‘Wadaadku waa inuu ku soo bandhigaa Rabbiga oo bixiyo dembi bixinta iyo gubashada.

## Tirinta 6:17

Waa inuu sidoo kale bixiyo wan sida allabari nabadeed Rabbiga, oo ay weheliyaan dambiil rooti aan khamiir lahayn; wadaadku waa inuu sidoo kale bixiyo allabariga daqiiqda iyo cabbitaanka.

## Tirinta 6:18

“‘Naasiri waa inuu markaas xiiraa timahooda quduuska ah iridda teendhada kulanka, oo ku ridaa timaha dabka hoostiisa allabariga nabadda.

## Tirinta 6:19

“‘Ka dib markii Naasiri uu xiiray timahooda quduuska ah, wadaadku waa inuu gacmahooda ku ridaa garabka wan la kariyey, hal rooti aan khamiir lahayn, iyo hal rooti aan khamiir lahayn oo dambiisha ku jira.

## Tirinta 6:20

Wadaadku waa inuu markaas u lulaa sida allabari lulid ah Rabbiga; waxay quduus yihiin oo wadaadka ayay u leeyihiin, oo ay la socdaan laabta la lulay iyo bowdada la bixiyey. Ka dib markaas, Naasiri wuxuu cabbi karaa khamri.

## Tirinta 6:21

“‘Tani waa sharciga Naasiri ee nidar u sameeya Rabbiga sida ay u hibeeyaan, oo ay weheliyaan wixii kale ee ay awoodaan. Waa inay fuliyaan nidarka ay sameeyeen, sida sharciga quduusnimadooda.’”

## Tirinta 6:22

Markaasaa Rabbigu la hadlay Muuse, isagoo leh,

## Tirinta 6:23

“La hadal Haaruun iyo wiilashiisa, oo ku dheh, ‘Sidan ayaad u barakayn doontaan reer binu Israa'iil. U dheh iyaga:

## Tirinta 6:24

“‘“Rabbigu ha idin barakeeyo oo ha idin ilaaliyo;

## Tirinta 6:25

Rabbigu ha idiin iftiimiyo wejigiisa oo ha idiin muujiyo naxariistiisa;

## Tirinta 6:26

Rabbigu ha idiin eego si naxariis leh oo ha idiin siiyo nabad.”’

## Tirinta 6:27

“Sidan ayay magacayga ugu yeeri doonaan reer binu Israa'iil, oo aniguna waan barakayn doonaa.”
