# Sabuurka 129

### Hees Korkacsi ah.

## Sabuurka 129:1

> “Marar badan ayay iga soo weerareen yaraantaydii,”  
> Yacquub ha yidhaahdo,

## Sabuurka 129:2

> “Marar badan ayay iga soo weerareen tan iyo yaraantaydii;  
> Hase yeeshee ima ay ka adkaan.

## Sabuurka 129:3

> Falaadhayaashu waxay ku faliyeen dhabarkayga;  
> Wayna dheereeyeen surimmadooda.”

## Sabuurka 129:4

> Eebbe waa xaq;  
> Wuxuu gooyay xadhkaha kuwa sharka leh.

---

## Sabuurka 129:5

> Kuwa oo dhan oo quudhsada Siyoon  
> Ha lagu ceebeeyo oo ha loo celiyo gadaal;

## Sabuurka 129:6

> Ha noqdeen sida cawska saqafka guryaha,  
> Kaas oo engega ka hor intaanu korin;

## Sabuurka 129:7

> Kaas oo goosaduhu aanay gacantiisa buuxin,  
> Mana buuxiyo gacmihiisa xidhaha xidhmooyinka;

## Sabuurka 129:8

> Mana odhanayaan kuwa ag maraya,  
> “Barakooyinka Eebbe ha idinku dul ahaadeen;  
> Magaca Eebbe ayaannu idinku barakaynaynaa.”
