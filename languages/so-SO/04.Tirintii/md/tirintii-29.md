# Tirintii Cutubka 29

## Tirintii 29:1
‘Bishii toddobaad, maalinta koowaad ee bisha, waxaad qaban doontaan kulan quduus ah oo ka fogaada shaqo kasta oo caadi ah. Waa inay noqotaa maalin loogu dhawaaqo buunno.

## Tirintii 29:2
Soo bandhiga allabari gubasho oo udgoon u ah Eebbe: dibi yar oo qura, wan qura, iyo toddoba wan oo lab ah oo sannad jir ah, dhammaantoodna aan iin lahayn.

## Tirintii 29:3
Dibiga la socda, soo bandhiga allabariga daqiiqda oo saddex meelood meel oo eefa oo bur fiican ah oo saliid lagu daray; wan la socda, laba meelood meel;

## Tirintii 29:4
iyo mid kasta oo toddobada wan ah, bixiya meelood meel.

## Tirintii 29:5
Ku dar horgale qura oo wan ah oo allabariga dembiga ah si aad u hesho kafaaraggud.

## Tirintii 29:6
Allabarayaashan waxaa lagu daray allabariga gubashada ee bil kasta iyo allabarigiisa daqiiqda, iyo allabariga gubashada ee joogtada ah iyo allabarigiisa daqiiqda iyo cabbitaanka, sida la faray. Waxay yihiin udgoon wanaagsan, allabari dab ah oo u ah Eebbe.

## Tirintii 29:7
‘Maalinta tobnaad ee bisha toddobaad, waxaad qaban doontaan kulan quduus ah oo is-hoosaysiiya; ha samaynin wax shaqo ah.

## Tirintii 29:8
Soo bandhiga allabari gubasho oo udgoon u ah Eebbe: dibi yar oo qura, wan qura, iyo toddoba wan oo lab ah oo sannad jir ah, dhammaantoodna aan iin lahayn.

## Tirintii 29:9
Dibiga la socda, soo bandhiga allabariga daqiiqda oo saddex meelood meel oo eefa oo bur fiican ah oo saliid lagu daray; wan la socda, laba meelood meel;

## Tirintii 29:10
iyo mid kasta oo toddobada wan ah, bixiya meelood meel.

## Tirintii 29:11
Ku dar horgale qura oo wan ah oo allabariga dembiga ah, marka lagu daro allabariga dembiga ee kafaaraggudka iyo allabariga gubashada ee joogtada ah iyo allabarigiisa daqiiqda iyo cabbitaanka.

## Tirintii 29:12
‘Maalinta shan iyo tobnaad ee bisha toddobaad, qabta kulan quduus ah oo ha samaynin wax shaqo ah. U dabaaldeg ciidda Eebbe toddoba maalmood.

## Tirintii 29:13
Soo bandhiga allabari gubasho, allabari dab ah oo udgoon u ah Eebbe: saddex iyo toban dibi oo yar, laba wan, iyo afar iyo toban wan oo lab ah oo sannad jir ah, dhammaantoodna aan iin lahayn.

## Tirintii 29:14
Mid kasta oo saddex iyo toban dibiga ah, soo bandhiga allabariga daqiiqda oo saddex meelood meel oo eefa oo bur fiican ah oo saliid lagu daray; mid kasta oo labada wan ah, laba meelood meel;

## Tirintii 29:15
iyo mid kasta oo afar iyo toban wan ah, bixiya meelood meel.

## Tirintii 29:16
Ku dar horgale qura oo wan ah oo allabariga dembiga ah, marka lagu daro allabariga gubashada ee joogtada ah iyo allabarigiisa daqiiqda iyo cabbitaanka.

## Tirintii 29:17
‘Maalinta labaad: laba iyo toban dibi oo yar, laba wan, iyo afar iyo toban wan oo lab ah oo sannad jir ah, dhammaantoodna aan iin lahayn.

## Tirintii 29:18
Dibiyada, wanka, iyo wanka la socda, soo bandhiga allabarigooda daqiiqda iyo cabbitaanka sida tirada loo cayimay.

## Tirintii 29:19
Ku dar horgale qura oo wan ah oo allabariga dembiga ah, marka lagu daro allabariga gubashada ee joogtada ah iyo allabarigiisa daqiiqda iyo cabbitaanka.

## Tirintii 29:20

## Tirintii 29:21
Dibiyada, wanka, iyo wanka la socda, soo bandhiga allabarigooda daqiiqda iyo cabbitaanka sida tirada loo cayimay.

## Tirintii 29:39
‘Marka lagu daro allabarigiinna ballanqaadka iyo allabarigiinna ikhtiyaariga ah, soo bandhiga kuwaan Eebbe ee ciidihiinna loo qoondeeyey: allabarigiinna gubashada, allabarigiinna daqiiqda, allabarigiinna cabbitaanka, iyo allabarigiinna nabadda.’

## Tirintii 29:40
Muuse wuxuu reer binu Israa'iil u gudbiyey wax kasta oo Eebbe ku amray.
